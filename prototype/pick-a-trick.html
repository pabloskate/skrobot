What changed

  1. Same robot, same stage, no cut. Today a flat icon wobbles in a white card, then the card turns into the plaza and a different, 3D robot. Now the pick happens on the attempt stage with the scene robot, and the attempt plays from the frame the pick ends on.
  2. It rolls while it thinks. The robot cruises in its roll-in pose with the street moving under it and tips its head up toward its options. It gets no new parts: the head tilts and the visor uses the open and happy faces it already has.
  3. The reel is its real bag. Names come from the robot’s set table in proportion to their weights, and tricks already set this game are left out. Swivel’s reel is mostly shuvits and Boomerang’s is mostly cabs. The reel ends on the engine’s own pick.
  4. It slows down so you can read it. The reel starts as a vertical blur and decelerates. Each name that settles ticks, and the robot nods at it. At the end it drifts a hair past the pick and clicks back.
  5. It calls it. The pick slams in the robot’s accent color under a strip of tape (“Sparky calls it”), and the visor smiles. The head nods down to the board, and the name lifts away before the pop so the trick has the whole sky.
  6. It takes its time. The reel runs about 2 s: a longer blur, then four or five names slow enough to read before the call. The trick starts at 2.30 s instead of 1.40 s, so the pop lands at 2.86 s instead of 1.96 s. Use Choosing to compare this with today’s timer and a longer option. How it maps to the code

The engine and the trick physics don’t change. What changes is when the trick is chosen and what the stage shows before its t = 0.

Keeps

  • Engine: the thinking stage and ROBOT_SET_CHOICE are unchanged. Only GameScreen’s timer changes, from 1400 ms to 2300 ms. chooseRobotTrick is unchanged. GameScreen calls it when thinking starts instead of when it ends, so the reel can land on the answer.
  • Trick physics: computeFrame, solveRig, rollAttempt, and the landed/missed flow are untouched. From the pop on, the stage is the attempt you already have.
  • Copy and a11y: the same “is picking a trick…” and “goes for a …” lines under the stage, still the live status. The reel is aria-hidden.
  • Out of tricks: cant keeps today’s card. There is nothing to reel.

Adds

  • TrickScene lead-in (scene only): a leadIn prop that renders negative time as cruising in the t = 0 pose, plus a head gaze (pitch/roll about the neck) that settles to zero before the pop. Tests pin that the last lead-in frame matches t = 0 and that bones keep their lengths.
  • PickReel in features/game: an HTML overlay above the stage. A pure reelTimeline(options, pick, seed) returns the rows and tick times. Tests pin that it ends on the pick, never shows a used trick, and locks 0.3 s before the timer fires.
  • GameScreen: the attempt panel mounts the stage during thinking, so there’s no avatar-to-stage swap. The toss/turn note stays up through the attempt (today it disappears at 1.40 s), so the text under the stage only changes height at the result.
  • Feedback: the tick and lock sounds go through the existing rpsSound / rpsVibrate hooks. Reduced motion skips the spin and fades the name in at the call.
