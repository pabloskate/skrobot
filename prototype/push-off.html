What happens

  1. Two pushes on flat ground. Each push is a dip, then a surge of speed. After that it's a small physics sim: rolling resistance slows it a little, and the camera leads it so there's room ahead.
  2. The cracks keep the beat. Each wheel clacks as it rolls over a sidewalk joint, and the board dips a pixel. Each front-wheel clack slams in the next letter, so the letters speed up as the robot does. An S.K.8 game gets three.
  3. An ollie, the first trick anyone learns. It crouches, the nose snaps up, and it levels out at the top. The jump follows real ballistic height, and the wheels don't clack while it's in the air. “Let's” tapes on at the peak, and the letter row flinches when it lands.
  4. Tail stop. It rocks back onto the tail and drags to a stop, kicking up dust behind it and leaving a scuff on the concrete. The camera settles first, so the robot slides into the center of the screen. Then the nose drops and the front wheels clack down.
  5. It says what to do next. “Sparky sets first. Land Sparky's trick back or take a letter.” The robot hops into stoked when it sets first and stays idle when you do. It gets no new body parts.
  6. Tap anywhere to skip. At the end it fades into the game underneath.

How it maps to the code

This replaces the inside of GameStartAnimation and keeps its props and contract. The motion is a small pure module that can be tested.

Keeps

  • Props: robot, playerFirst, onComplete. It still mounts from RpsPanel when you press Start.
  • RobotAvatar poses: idle and stoked only, with the same drawing. The ollie and tail stop are rotations of it, not new art.
  • Engine: the START dispatch is unchanged, so the game's own “You won the toss” note still greets you underneath.

Adds

  • pushOff.ts: a fixed-step integrator that returns a timeline (position, height, speed, crack clacks, camera). Tests can pin “one letter per front-wheel clack, no clacks in the air, stops centered, finishes under 3.5 s”.
  • One SVG plus requestAnimationFrame, transforms only. The type and stickers are CSS.
  • A letters prop from lettersForFormat. The ollie waits for the last letter, so S.K.8 games are shorter.
  • Tap to skip, and reduced motion shows the settled frame for 1.2 s, then fades.
