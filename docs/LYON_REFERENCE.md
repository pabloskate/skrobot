# Lyon 25 reference

## References inspected

- The user's dimensions, October 2026: **14.76 feet high, 21.29 feet long**. These are the authoritative gameplay dimensions: **4.498848 m down, 6.489192 m horizontally**. Length is treated as horizontal nosing-to-last-riser distance, consistent with the existing landmark layouts, not the diagonal staircase length.
- [Sxkcb, *Lyon 25*, photographed 4 February 2023](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lyon_25.jpg). The full-resolution photograph was visually inspected. It looks along the building from above the bottom landing and establishes the pale retaining bank, the park promenade above it, the long upper courtyard, glazing, balconies, overhang, secondary distant stairs and spiral stair.
- [LocusXovier, *Lyon25Scale*, photographed 5 November 2025](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lyon25Scale.png). The 1280-pixel photograph was visually inspected. People provide scale, and the oblique angle shows the 25 uninterrupted steps, offset joints in their long stone pieces, small square courtyard pavers, tall retained edge and the building-side bracketed handrail.
- [RideMyPark, *The Lyon 25*](https://ridemypark.com/fr/spots/the-lyon-25), [bottom-up photo inspected](https://api.ridemypark.com/web/v1/img?u=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.ridemypark.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Flisting-uploads%2Fgallery%2F2022%2F03%2FFD31BBC3-9AFE-4303-81FC-566380CE772D.jpeg&w=1280). The pale, stained risers, orange horizontal terracotta panels, grey uprights, deep upper cantilever with light soffit, and tall plain sidewall are clear from the landing.
- [Thrasher Magazine, *Classics: Ali Boulala "Sorry"*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8bo6Ckw2pqM&t=267s). The actual video was played and frames in the Lyon sequence around **4:27–4:40** inspected, particularly the bottom-up view at 4:38. This verifies the historic open skating configuration and the orange crosswall, spiral escape stair and tree at the far end of the run-up. [Thrasher's *Jaws vs the Lyon 25*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4GFIXrybfKg) was located but playback requires age-confirmed sign-in in the available browser; it was not claimed as watched.
- [Wikimedia Commons' Lyon 25 category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Lyon_25) lists 12 m width, 6.5 m length and 4.5 m height. The rounded length/height corroborate the user's values; the width is used as a secondary reference, not represented as a fresh survey.

## Layout and fidelity

The scale is **29 world units per foot**, shared with El Toro and the riders. The takeoff lip is `x=0, y=0`; positive x travels downhill. The orange convention-centre building occupies **positive z** and the high pale retaining wall **negative z**. Looking up from the landing, the building is on the left. Rider stance never mirrors the architecture.

There are **25 separate risers and 24 intervening treads**. Equal heights and spacing are a modeling assumption: each riser is 0.5904 feet / 17.995392 cm, each tread is 0.8870833 feet / 27.0383 cm. A long, level paved courtyard leads to the drop, and the lower courtyard remains flat through the rollout. There is **no centre handrail and no pair of freestanding stair rails**. The lone stair handrail hugs the terracotta wall on brackets, and is not exposed as a grind route.

The modeled recognition features are the full-width pale stone flight; narrow dark stone joints and uneven rain streaks; small square paving; long orange horizontal terracotta rainscreen panels with grey structural seams; ground-floor glass entries away from the stair flight; an upper gallery with four horizontal grey guardrail bars; the orange cantilever above a pale soffit; and the tall pale retaining sidewall below a sloping panelled bank, with a railed park promenade above it. At the distant end of the run-up are the orange crosswall, a recessed door, small spiral escape stair, benches and a tree bed. More trees stand on the park side above the bank.

Only the user's total rise/run are treated as exact. Individual step dimensions are derived from those totals. Width comes from the secondary spot record. Building height and bay spacing, courtyard lengths, bank slope/height, gallery levels, railing diameters, tree/bench positions and the spiral stair are visual estimates. They are documented constants or deterministic geometry, not claimed measured reproductions. Distant building roof curves, signage lettering and the secondary access staircase are simplified or omitted. The scene follows the open historical skating configuration; contemporary barriers and tactile anti-skate additions are omitted deliberately.

Three tripod views stay inside the narrow open courtyard: the lower filming position looking up the flight, a lower side position close to the retaining wall, and the upper corner looking down. The moving crane is constrained between the retaining wall and the building's overhang. The material and shadow treatment reuse the existing daylight landmark renderer.

Photographs and video remain external research references. No downloaded source image or video is shipped as a texture or app asset. Tests check the exact requested dimensions, all 25 risers, floor/physics agreement, the full-height retaining wall, absence of invented center rails, and finite merged geometry within the mobile vertex budget.
