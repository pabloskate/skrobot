# Virtual location presentation

Skate Robot keeps the riding challenges while reducing unnecessary use of real
business branding, artwork and distinctive surrounding architecture. These are
presentation changes, not a claim of legal clearance.

- **Roof to Bank** replaces the public name Sunset Car Wash. The business name,
  slogan and circular badges are removed; the canopy is sage and cream.
- **25 Stair** replaces the public name Lyon 25. The 25-step riding dimensions
  remain, with original low-rise buildings and a low rear seat wall in place of
  the convention-centre facade, gallery, cantilever and spiral staircase.
- **Triangle Bank** replaces the public name Miami Triangle. The bank's riding
  face and edges remain. Memorial inscriptions, emblem, artist lettering and
  tower are removed. The deck has a narrower rectangular rear, a pointed nose
  and low garden seating instead of the memorial's equilateral platform and
  triangular planting bed. The plaza uses one stone tile color with subtle
  grout lines, without patchwork tile colors or decorative stains.
- **Hollywood 16** keeps its name and riding obstacles; its auditorium wall is
  plain rather than reproducing the portrait mural.

The animation registry owns display names used by both explorers and video
filenames. Public URLs use `roof-to-bank`, `25-stair` and `triangle-bank`.
Historical set IDs remain internal renderer keys and are accepted in old links;
opening or sharing one produces the current neutral URL. Research notes retain
the original names and sources as provenance.

The explorer feature owns `/about`, the product and virtual-location notice.
Dream Tricks, Trick Explorer and Settings link to it. The notice explains that
Skate Robot is independent, depictions do not imply affiliation or endorsement,
scenes may use historical or redesigned layouts, and virtual scenes do not
establish current real-world conditions, access or permission to skate.
